About Me

Welcome to my personal homepage! I'm someone who really really enjoysspending time on my interests during my free time.

When I'm not busy with school or other responsibilities, I usually enjoy building model kits, playing badminton, gaming, taking photos, or watching movies (even the bad ones yes).

Things I Enjoy

Here are a few of my favourite ways to spend my free time.

1. Building Plastic Model Kits

I enjoy putting model kits together piece by piece. It takes patience and a bit of care, but seeing all the parts come together makes the effort worth it. It's kind of like building with LEGO, except the pieces and tools need a little more attention.

2. Badminton

Badminton is a fun way to stay active. I've especially enjoyed playing with my family, including my grandfather and my brother.

Read a badminton guide

3. Video Games

I've enjoyed video games since I was just a little kid. I especially like single-player games because of their stories, characters, and worlds.

4. Photography

I like taking pictures of things that catch my attention, especially scenery and moments during events. I don't have any fancy equipment but its still a ton of fun.

5. Watching Movies

Watching movies is one of my favourite ways to relax after a long day. I enjoy films with interesting stories or great visuals. If a movie keeps me entertained from beginning to end, there's a really good chance I'll probahly watch it again.

A Quote I Like

One line from a game that has stuck with me is:

“Do not be sorry. Be better.”

This quote is from Kratos in the God of War game. I like it because it reminds me that learning from mistakes and improving matters more than just feeling bad about them.
